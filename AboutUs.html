FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How does Tunewave’s white-label music distribution solution work? ▼
Tunewave lets music businesses run their own branded distribution service. You can deliver music to global streaming platforms, manage royalties, and control channels—all under your own brand.
Is Tunewave suitable for all music genres? ▼
Yes, Tunewave supports all music genres.
Does Tunewave handle royalties? ▼
Yes, Tunewave provides royalty management for all distributed music.
Do I retain ownership of my music? ▼
Absolutely. You retain full ownership of your music.
Who can use Tunewave Label? ▼
Independent artists, small labels, and music distributors who want to manage and grow their music business.
How do I get started? ▼
You can sign up on our platform and start distributing music immediately.
Can Tunewave help with marketing? ▼
Yes, we offer marketing support to promote your music globally.
Does Tunewave offer support after launch? ▼
Yes, our support team is available to help you anytime.
